Partner Card

Partner Card.dc.html

Directory tile. At rest: brand-tinted bed, mark centred, positioning line in the white strip, icon-only arrow. On hover the bed fills the card, the mark drops into the top-left pad, the copy and text link fade in. Hit box is static, so the state cannot flicker at the edge.

Value Card

Value Card.dc.html

Proposition tile. Icon top-right over drifting rings, heading bottom-left; on hover the heading rises and the copy opens beneath it. Light and dark tones; the light one carries a tinted ramp that turns on hover. reveal="always" pins the copy open — same geometry as the hovered state — for quiet sections where the description is the content.

Speaker Card

Speaker Card.dc.html

Person card: square portrait tile (blue or tone="pink"), name and role. Two variants — linkedin hangs one icon button beside the name and the whole card links to the profile; contacts puts a rule under the name and the inner pages' share row beneath it at size="xs", round: email, Telegram, LinkedIn. Pass the portrait as photo, or a slot-id for an editor drop. The name and slot props are name-text / slot-id — name= is the dc-import selector.
