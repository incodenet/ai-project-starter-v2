Molecules · Case Card

The case study card

One component behind every case tile: the split card approved on Services v2, as Case Card.dc.html. Variant A is the Success Spotlight card from Services v2 verbatim; Variant B is the supporting card in its two-column mode, with the metric plaque bleeding to the card edges. Pages import it — the card is edited in one place for the whole site.

01, Variants

Featured, standard & compact

A split card: text side plus a coloured metric panel. The whole card is one link. The result figure counts up when it scrolls into view.

Variant A — featured, full width

variant="featured"

Deep purple surface with the dotted texture; white client logo, tag pills, title, description and the white pill button. The metric panel is inset on the right with the ring-pulse circles and the 3D arrow (it tilts on hover).

02, Tone

The service palette

The metric panel uses a fixed six-tone palette of bright brand-hue gradients — violet (Strategy), blue (Setup), teal (Operation), lime (Retention), amber (Consultation), indigo (Migration) — and the same hue carries into the tag pills and the link. tone="auto" derives it from the first service tag. The featured panel is always the violet metric gradient.
