Design System · Colour

The Colour System

One violet carries the brand, two inks carry the text, and every value is a token in hi-tokens.css. Use the variable, never the raw hex.

01, Brand palette

Violet, ink, dark

Two violets: the deeper one for light backgrounds, the brighter one on dark. Cyan appears only as a second data colour, never as a brand tone.

Rules

How to use colour

  1. Always the token, never the literal — var(--c-accent), not #7B52C7.
  2. Accent picks its tone from the background: --c-accent on light, --c-accent-bright or --c-lav on dark.
  3. Softer text is alpha of the same ink, not a new grey.
  4. Two background colours per page at most; depth comes from the gradients above.
  5. A new colour is added to tokens/hi-tokens.css first, then to this sheet — a page never invents one.
