Image Guide

One file per item, with the size to upload and the weight to stay under. Every number here was measured on the live pages at screen widths from 375 to 1920 pixels.

News

News image

1800 × 1200

3:2 WebP under 250 KB

One image per news item, used on the news page, on the cards and at the top of the article.

Keep the subject in the middle. The edges of the frame are trimmed.

Need to know

16:9 on the article page

Article page

The article page crops wider, trimming the top and the bottom.

4% is lost on hover

Cards on hover

Pointing at a card zooms the picture, so the outer edge leaves the view.

Blog

Blog image

1800 × 1200

3:2 WebP under 250 KB

One image per blog post, used on the blog page, on the cards and at the top of the article.

The whole frame is always shown, nothing is trimmed.

Events

Event image

2400 × 1600

3:2 WebP under 350 KB

One image per event, used on the card and as the full screen background on the event page.

Keep the subject at middle height and a little right of centre.

Need to know

Upcoming
18–20
JAN

Listing card

A category label and the date sit in the top corners. Keep faces away from them.

Event page, desktop

The title runs over the left half on a dark gradient. Keep the subject to the right of it.

a phone shows 30%

Event page, phone

A phone cuts the sides away and shows only the middle strip of the photo.

Company

Company story image

1200 × 1600

3:4 WebP under 300 KB

One photograph per chapter of the story timeline on the Company page.

Upload it upright, and leave free space above and below the subject. On a narrow screen the photo turns landscape and those strips are cut away.

Need to know

Desktop, 3:4

Upright beside the text. The whole photo is shown.

Tablet and phone, 4:3

The slot turns landscape, so the top and bottom 22% are cut away.

Meet the Team

Team hero image

1200 × 1600

3:4 WebP under 300 KB

One photograph of the whole team, beside the heading at the top of the page.

Upload it upright, with free space above and below the group. On a phone the slot becomes a square and both strips are cut away.

Need to know

Desktop and tablet, 3:4

Upright beside the heading. The whole photo is shown.

Phone, 1:1

Below 640px the slot is square, so the top and bottom 12% are trimmed.
